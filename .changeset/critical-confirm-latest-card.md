`ApprovalCard` and `ToolApprovalBatch` wait for the person on a critical action. The first press stays armed, with no time limit, until the second press, Escape, or focus leaving the card, instead of resetting after 4 seconds, so a screen reader user who confirms after hearing "Press approve again to confirm" no longer arms it again. An armed press belongs to the arguments it would approve: new ones from the agent, an edit made after it, or another call joining Approve all need a new first press. Approve all's group takes the focus of a click, as the card does, so in Safari, where a click doesn't focus the button, focus leaving it disarms it too. The second click of a double click no longer counts as a press: a double click on Approve or Approve all arms a critical call and does not approve it. With `globalShortcut` on several pending cards, as `AgentMessage`'s `approvalProps` gives parallel calls, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter answers one card, the one the keys are pressed in or else the latest to ask, not the first card that mounted. When a newer card takes the keys over, a press they armed on a critical card from outside it is dropped, so it can't be confirmed later by a single press.
